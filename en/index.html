Frequently asked questions

What services do you provide?

Video production, drone filming, photography and web development. We work with individual or corporate clients - from small businesses to industrial facilities. We can take care of everything at once or just what is needed.

Can you make both a video and a website?

Yes. The combination of these services is particularly suitable for real estate projects, start-ups or businesses undergoing renovation. One team will do the work, maintain a consistent style, with less hassle for you.

Do you offer drone filming services?

Providing professional aerial drone filming for advertising, real estate projects, events, corporate videos and area visualisation. Compliance with current Lithuanian aviation and flight safety requirements.

Are photo and video services provided together?

We provide comprehensive photo and video services to produce seamless visual content for advertising, websites and social communication. This reduces costs and ensures consistency of style.

In which cities are video filming services available?

Video, photo and drone filming services are available in Klaipėda and throughout Lithuania. Work is carried out both in major cities and regions. We also carry out projects in foreign countries.

What is the cost of the service?

Depends on the scope of the project. Smaller video projects start at ~400€, medium projects at 1000-2000€. The price of a website depends on the type and functionality. The best way is to write a brief description of the project and you will get a custom proposal within 24h.
